Privacy Policy

Effective date: October 30, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Arvello Labs Inc ("we", "us", or "our") handles information in connection with Buzzle Beads: Color Puzzle, our TikTok mini game, and this legal information website. This policy applies only to the TikTok edition and this website. Editions distributed on other platforms and TikTok's own services are governed by their respective privacy policies.

1. Information used by the game

Progress and preferences. The game uses storage provided by the TikTok mini game platform to save your progress, unfinished puzzles, item balances, collection unlocks, display arrangements and backgrounds, and sound, vibration and language settings. This lets you continue playing and preserves your choices. The current version does not upload these saves to a game server operated by us or provide our own cross-device cloud save service.

Basic system information. The game reads platform-provided system and language information to run correctly and select the appropriate language. The current version does not request access to your contacts, photos, microphone, camera or precise location.

Support messages. If you contact us, we receive your email address and any information you choose to include, such as a description of a problem or a screenshot. We use this information to answer your request, troubleshoot issues and handle privacy or safety concerns. Please do not send passwords or unnecessary sensitive information.

2. Accounts, advertising and analytics

The current TikTok release does not require a separate Buzzle Beads account, exchange TikTok login credentials with our servers, or request your TikTok profile information. Game-operated advertising placements and a separate game analytics upload service are not currently enabled. We will update this policy and provide any notice or choice required by law before introducing materially different data processing.

TikTok may process information about your account, device and use of its platform, including your interaction with mini games, under its own policies. The statements above describe our game's implementation and do not mean that TikTok processes no information. Please review the TikTok Privacy Policy applicable to your region and the privacy controls in TikTok.

3. How information is used and shared

We use the information described above to provide the game, remember your choices, respond to requests, maintain security and meet applicable legal obligations. We do not sell the personal information we receive through this version of the game or use it for cross-context behavioral advertising.

Providers that deliver our email and website hosting services may process the information needed to provide those services. We may disclose information when legally required or when reasonably necessary to address fraud or security incidents, protect rights, or respond to a lawful request. We limit disclosures to what is appropriate for the relevant purpose.

4. This website

These legal pages are hosted on GitHub Pages. We do not add analytics scripts, advertising trackers, sign-in forms or cookies to these pages. GitHub processes visitors' IP addresses for security purposes and may process routine request information to deliver the site. See the GitHub Privacy Statement. Following an external link takes you to a service governed by its own policies.

5. Retention and security

Saved progress and settings remain in platform-provided storage until that data is cleared or removed by you or the platform. Clearing mini game or app data can permanently remove progress; available controls depend on TikTok and your device. We cannot restore a local save that we do not hold.

We retain support correspondence only for as long as needed to resolve the request and for related security, recordkeeping or legal requirements. Hosting and platform providers manage their own logs under their policies. We use reasonable safeguards appropriate to the information we handle, but no storage or transmission method is completely secure.

6. Your choices and rights

You can change available game preferences in the game and manage platform permissions through TikTok or your device. To request access, correction or deletion of personal information held by us, or to raise a privacy concern, email minigames@blazedragonsoul.xyz. We may need information sufficient to verify that the request relates to you. We cannot directly access or erase information held solely by TikTok or local game storage on your device, but can explain the relevant distinction.

Depending on your location and applicable law, you may also have rights to object to or restrict processing, obtain a portable copy, withdraw consent where processing relies on consent, appeal a decision, or complain to a data protection authority. Contact us to exercise applicable rights. Where a legal basis is required, we rely on providing the service you request, legitimate interests in support and security, legal obligations, or consent where required.

7. International processing

The platform, website hosting and communication services may process information in countries other than your own. Where applicable law requires safeguards for transfers for which we are responsible, we use those safeguards. TikTok and GitHub describe their own processing locations and safeguards in their respective privacy policies.

8. Children and younger players

This mini game is intended for people who meet TikTok's age requirements and any higher minimum age required in their region. It is not directed to children under 13. Where parental or guardian permission is required, obtain it before playing. If you believe a child has provided personal information to us inappropriately, contact us so we can investigate and take appropriate action.

9. Updates and contact

We will post changes here and update the effective date. If a change requires additional notice or consent, we will provide it as required. For privacy questions or requests, contact Arvello Labs Inc at minigames@blazedragonsoul.xyz.
